# Azoth v0.3.0-rc.5 — evolving intent and bounded decisions

`v0.3.0-rc.5` is a public working-thesis refinement. It preserves the runtime,
project evidence, and executable-proof boundaries of `v0.3.0-rc.4` while
clarifying how a durable agentic system should represent evolving human intent
and decide when to explore, act, stop, or return authority to a person. It does
not retarget or alter any earlier release-candidate tag.

## What changed

- Added a three-resolution entry to the working thesis so readers can reach the
  plain-language, engineering, or research claim without flattening the deeper
  document.
- Made the direction of generalization explicit: agent systems are the practical
  origin and strongest evidence; broader engineering under uncertainty remains
  a cautious working hypothesis.
- Clarified that an intent anchor is a versioned representation of a person or
  collective's evolving intent, not the objective or subject itself.
- Made disagreement, current decision authority, superseding revisions, and
  partial identifiability explicit without claiming a uniquely measurable
  outcome distribution.
- Added practical stopping conditions for conflicting authority, insufficient
  evidence, non-discriminating feedback, proxy gaming, and disproportionate
  iteration cost.
- Added the consequence-relative rule `search broadly, act narrowly`: learn
  through discriminating reversible probes, then choose the simplest action
  that remains acceptable, observable, recoverable, and accountable.
- Extended the counterarguments, falsifiable questions, and research agenda so
  evolving intent cannot become an after-the-fact excuse for failure.

## Implemented proof retained

This update changes documentation only. It does not expand runtime behavior or
the portable proof claim. The public slice remains limited to deterministic
effect-aware routing, compact source-referenced context, explicit authority and
stopping state, and a four-case no-write rehearsal covered by 30 focused public
tests.

## Validation contract

The exact release-bearing extraction must pass the extraction tests, all 30
allowlisted portable public tests, the fail-closed public validator, privacy and
credential scanning, local-link and heading-anchor checks, Ruff,
product-release smoke, and Bash consumer installation/no-write rehearsal.
PowerShell smoke may be reported unavailable when no PowerShell runtime exists.

Publication remains a separate explicit gate after semantic review of the exact
extracted tree. Existing tags remain immutable.
