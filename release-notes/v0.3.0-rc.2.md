# Azoth v0.3.0-rc.2 — braided evidence and durable-system inquiry

`v0.3.0-rc.2` is an unreleased candidate for a cohesive, evidence-led public
surface around Azoth’s intent-to-outcome engineering inquiry. It does not
retarget or alter the immutable `v0.3.0-rc.1` tag.

The candidate separates four public responsibilities:

- the README introduces the engineering question and routes the project;
- *Narrow Success, Broad Failure* records historical work and the findings it
  exposed;
- *Intent-to-Outcome Engineering* develops a qualified working thesis; and
- *Executable Proof: Routing, Context, and Authority* explains the operator
  experience while preserving the boundary of the tested public slice.

## What changed

- Rewrote the README as a natural project hub. It now begins with the question
  of where durable intelligence lives when a thread is one bounded work pulse,
  shows the intent-to-outcome loop, and separates **Portable proof**, **Root
  workshop evidence**, and **Working direction**.
- Rebuilt *Narrow Success, Broad Failure* as a braided chronology. The origin is
  operational reporting semantics and BigQuery migration, followed by reuse in
  local data requests, internal framework composition and promotion,
  architectural overgrowth and contraction, a later agent-operations case, and
  the independent Azoth synthesis.
- Expanded each historical stage into a project mini-case with problem,
  approach, implementation, value, and a separately labeled observation or
  engineering inference.
- Deepened *Intent-to-Outcome Engineering: A Working Thesis on Durable Agentic
  Systems*. It distinguishes model invocation, bounded work pulse, and the
  intentionally unnamed durable whole; keeps `node` provisional; and treats
  “system as agent” as an analogy rather than settled terminology.
- Added qualified models of alignment-signal propagation and residual entropy.
  The document explicitly avoids treating them as calibrated quantities,
  recognizes that useful work can expose uncertainty, and gives evidence,
  deterministic checks, observed outcomes, recovery, and human authority
  distinct roles in resolving it.
- Made loop expansion conditional on semantic grounding, observability,
  recovery, and authority rather than presenting larger autonomous loops as
  inherently better.
- Expanded the executable proof around the user experience: intent intake,
  discovery, research sufficiency, initiative and roadmap formation, routed
  work pulses, evidence ledgers, evaluation, bounded replay, protected gates,
  and closeout.
- Added qualitative root-campaign evidence for product-strategy support, real
  stage-evidence enforcement, and evaluator-triggered repair, without exporting
  private receipts or claiming one complete autonomous product-management
  product.
- Added reciprocal navigation among the case study, thesis, proof, and README
  without using any document to expand another’s implementation claim.
- Preserved the working `Personal Harness OS` label only in provenance-bearing
  file and API paths.
- Preserved the thesis in fail-closed required paths, extraction checks, and
  product-release smoke validation.

## Implemented proof retained

- Effect-aware routing through `HarnessRequest`, `HarnessDecision`, and
  `RouteCapsule`.
- Compact, source-referenced context assembly with raw-memory filtering and
  explicit missing-source warnings.
- A portable four-case rehearsal surface that checks route, authority, warning,
  and repository no-write expectations.
- Thirty focused public tests for the selected routing, context, rehearsal, and
  personal-knowledge contracts.

## Claim boundary

The case study records project experience and derived findings. The thesis is a
qualified and revisable engineering model. Root workshop capabilities and
campaign observations explain the wider operating experience. Only the
portable routing, context, authority, and no-write rehearsal slice is claimed
as implemented and tested in the extracted public candidate.

The operational reporting migration, Local Requests Data workflow, internal
Agentic Framework, and GloBuddy are provenance-distinct historical projects.
They contributed evidence and signals; they are not Azoth deployments or
retroactive implementations of the thesis.

This candidate does not claim a complete durable intent-to-outcome system,
autonomous research-to-delivery product, installer, cross-host parity, external
adoption, or production deployment of Azoth.

## Validation evidence

The exact release-bearing extraction must pass:

- all 30 allowlisted portable public tests;
- the fail-closed public boundary validator;
- Ruff checks over the extracted public Python surface;
- privacy, credential-pattern, manifest, release-evidence, and local-reference
  validation;
- Mermaid rendering and Markdown layout inspection; and
- a fresh no-write rehearsal against the extracted tree.

Exact source provenance is emitted into the extracted `azoth.yaml` manifest.
Publication remains gated on semantic review of the complete extracted tree and
explicit human approval for the public commit, annotated tag, push, and
prerelease.
