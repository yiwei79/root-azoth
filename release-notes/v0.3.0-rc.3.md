# Azoth v0.3.0-rc.3 — conversation-to-outcome narrative amendment

`v0.3.0-rc.3` is an unreleased amendment candidate for Azoth's public entry
surface. It preserves the implementation and evidence boundaries of
`v0.3.0-rc.2` while restoring the concrete problem-to-solution opening that was
lost during the durable-system revision. It does not retarget or alter the
immutable `v0.3.0-rc.1` or `v0.3.0-rc.2` tags.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the README opening around a familiar failure in AI-assisted work: a
  thread can produce a useful local result while the wider intent, evidence,
  authority boundary, or stopping condition fades.
- Added a clean comparison between **conversation-centred continuity** and
  **outcome-centred continuity**, focused only on why the two operating modes
  diverge.
- Restored a separate **Azoth in one view** feedback loop so the entry surface
  still explains durable continuity, bounded work pulses, evidence, observed
  effects, evaluation, recovery, and human authority as one operating system.
- Clarified that conversation remains a useful interaction surface. The
  engineering objection is to making a transcript the sole plan, memory,
  policy, evidence ledger, and outcome state for durable work.
- Moved the question of where durable intelligence lives after the concrete
  failure and contrast, so the ontology is introduced as a consequence of a
  recognizable problem rather than as prerequisite vocabulary.
- Preserved operational-data semantic reconstruction as the historical origin
  of the inquiry. Conversation-centred agent use is the reader-facing entrance
  to the problem, not a retroactive origin story.
- Added a thesis-opening failure-and-response figure that moves from local
  success with broad drift to a persistent intent anchor, bounded work pulse,
  evidence, and durable-state update. It provides a rigorous visual entry
  without replacing the thesis's deeper argument.
- Kept the braided case study and executable proof focused on their distinct
  jobs: retrospective evidence and bounded implementation respectively.

## Implemented proof retained

The amendment does not expand runtime behavior or the portable proof claim.
The public slice remains limited to deterministic effect-aware routing, compact
source-referenced context, explicit authority and stopping state, and a
four-case no-write rehearsal covered by 30 focused public tests.

## Validation contract

The exact release-bearing extraction must pass the extraction tests, all 30
allowlisted portable public tests, the fail-closed public validator, privacy and
credential scanning, local-link and heading-anchor checks, Ruff, product-release
smoke, no-write rehearsal, and Mermaid rendering and layout inspection.

Publication remains a separate explicit gate after semantic review of the exact
extracted tree. No public repository mutation, tag, push, or release is implied
by this amendment candidate.
