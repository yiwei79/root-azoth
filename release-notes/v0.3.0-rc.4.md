# Azoth v0.3.0-rc.4 — operational-systems evidence expansion

`v0.3.0-rc.4` is a public case-study evidence update. It preserves the runtime
implementation and validation boundary of `v0.3.0-rc.3` while strengthening
the grounded account of the operational systems that informed the independent
Azoth inquiry. It does not retarget or alter any earlier release-candidate tag.

## What changed

- Reframed the opening case-study diagram around the transferable method:
  recover ambiguous meaning, bound execution and authority, verify the real
  effect, and promote only earned learning.
- Added a SupplyOps decision view that distinguishes semantic reconstruction
  and explicit acceptance contracts from the retained BigQuery, publication,
  readback, and rollback implementation view.
- Added the 3PL administration redesign as a complete operating-system case:
  lifecycle modelling, technical/manual responsibility boundaries, executable
  procedures, staged adoption, independent Real-Time Operations ownership, and
  live-state verification.
- Expanded GloBuddy into two complementary views: the production product and
  outcome loop, and the higher-level implementation/effect-control design for
  authoritative context, deterministic eligibility, reservation,
  deduplication, provider outcomes, and reconciliation.
- Clarified that the GloBuddy ME behavior-and-release path is an inspectable
  slice of the unified production product rather than the whole product or a
  deployment of Azoth.
- Added technical-judgment recaps and explicit public claim boundaries so the
  document can support both a concise discussion and a deeper architecture
  explanation without overstating causality or implementation scope.

## Implemented proof retained

This update does not expand runtime behavior or the portable proof claim. The
public slice remains limited to deterministic effect-aware routing, compact
source-referenced context, explicit authority and stopping state, and a
four-case no-write rehearsal covered by 30 focused public tests.

## Validation contract

The exact release-bearing extraction must pass the extraction tests, all 30
allowlisted portable public tests, the fail-closed public validator, privacy and
credential scanning, local-link and heading-anchor checks, Ruff,
product-release smoke, no-write rehearsal, and Mermaid rendering and layout
inspection.

Publication remains a separate explicit gate after semantic review of the
exact extracted tree. Existing tags remain immutable.
